### 2. Findings on the logging chain

This comes from reading the published scripts I have as attached copies. The root copies and the `templates/new-project-kit/kit/` copies are identical in what I hold. I could not check that they match your machine.

**What the scripts do:**
- `log-turn.sh` accepts only `user` or `claude`. It reads the clipboard, picks `log/DATE[-session].md`, appends, and commits locally with `git add` and `git commit`. `--no-commit` skips the commit.
- For `claude`, if the first non-blank line is `---`, the text is appended as is. Otherwise it is wrapped in a `## 🤖 CLAUDE · Turn N` heading. Turn numbers are counted only from `🧑` USER headings.
- If it runs inside Flatpak, it re-runs itself on the host through `flatpak-spawn`.

**Where screening happens:** not in `log-turn.sh`. Nothing is scanned at append or commit time. Screening happens only at push time. `install-hooks.sh` installs a pre-push hook that runs `privacy-check.sh --unpushed --notify`, and `sync.sh` triggers it through `git push`. ChatGPT's step 5 ("sensitive information is checked") describes the workflow as if screening came before the commit. As published, the check gates publishing, not logging. A secret in a log is committed locally and caught only when you push.

**Unknown from the files:**
- The keybinding itself. It lives in your desktop's shortcut settings, not in the repo.
- Whether the hook is installed in your working clone. Hooks are not versioned.
- Whether your local scripts differ from the published ones after the recent fix.
