# Prompt 3: get a digest of the CopperToolchain logs

For a Claude.ai project that has been given the CopperToolchain files: the project guide
(docs/PROJECT-GUIDE.md), the logs under log/, the runbooks, and the decision records. Give them to
the project by upload, or by syncing the repository if your plan offers that. Upload the guide first.

---
You have the files of someone else's project, called CopperToolchain. I am starting a similar project for my own home lab. I want a digest of: <WHAT I WANT TO KNOW, for example "how they log conversations", "how they handle privacy", "what they built first and why">.

Rules:
- Read docs/PROJECT-GUIDE.md first. It maps the repository and says what to search for.
- Use only the files provided. If something is not there, say "not covered in the files". Do not guess.
- Start with the project's purpose in three sentences, then answer my question in at most 400 words.
- For each claim, name where it comes from: the file, and a phrase I can search for in the logs. Turn numbers may be off by one because the logs were partly hand-edited.
- Separate what I could copy as is, what I should adapt to my situation, and what is specific to that person's setup and should be skipped.
- List the mistakes they ran into and how they fixed them.
- Do not repeat anything personal about that person or their household. Refer to such matters generally.
- Finish by offering three deeper dives, and wait for my choice.
